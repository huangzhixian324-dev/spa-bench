## eTOC blurb (<= 50 words, two sentences, third person)

Benchmark rankings in immunotherapy transcriptomics depend more on methodological
choices than on algorithms. Isolating response definition, feature selection, and
endpoint circularity across 196 patients shows that gene-defined surrogate
endpoints inflate overlapping methods, and a null-referenced circularity score
flags such endpoints before benchmarking begins.
